## Cross-cutting observations for HavenOS replication
1. Two generations of hiring pipeline exist: legacy (application/application evaluation/written and pi test/interview/wont offer/hired/fired/left haven/subtask started/[shortlisted]/subtask done/complete — statuses double as post-hire employment state) and modern (new applications/sourced or pending loom/shortlisted/interviewing/hired/not a fit/complete + emoji-star interview scorecards + structured email/url/text form fields). The modern EA / Ops (Jordan) + Revenue Manager 25 + Owner Relations AM shape is the best model.
2. Nearly all hiring tasks are created by ClickBot from ClickUp Forms (task name = applicant, description = Q&A dump, custom fields = form answers; Tendwell Ops Manager 26 tasks even carry task_type "form_response"). Custom fields are duplicated across lists with inconsistent names/types (e.g. two "Loom Video" fields, "Speedtest" twice, misnamed "OOptions:*" field shared folder-wide).
3. Status misuse patterns to normalize: statuses-as-departments (Company Forms & Templates Used, Orientation), statuses-as-employment-state (HR Employee Log, Employee Information Form, hiring lists), and a corrupted status set (Executive Assistant with person names).
4. Most of the space is archival: of 42 lists, only ~6 show activity in the last ~90 days (Employee Onboarding, Employee Offboarding, HR Employee List, Runner, Bookkeeper 2025, borderline). Payroll Requests, Recurring Tasks List, 📋 Form, Video Ad Editor, Dispatch/PM Training List are empty shells.
5. Sensitive data present: pay Rate (currency) on Employee Onboarding, salary expectation fields on hiring lists, incident dates/personality labels and performance ratings in HR Employee Log, applicant PII (emails, phones, addresses, locations) throughout. Values were not copied into this audit.
